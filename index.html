    How to play
    • Every turn has two phases. In the fire phase heat spreads, things ignite, smoke drifts and the structure weakens. Fire never spreads during your phase.
    • Trucks arrive over several turns. When one is on scene, click Park, then click a road or driveway tile. R (or right-click) turns it a quarter at a time; the arrow and white headlights mark the front, red lights the back. Engines are 2×5 tiles, ladder trucks 2×7.
    • Water comes from the engine's tank, which arrives with 20 units. Every spray uses one. The tank only refills from a hydrant: pull a yellow 5″ supply line (LDH) from the engine, walk it next to a hydrant and click the hydrant. Hooking up takes 5 AP of work: cap off, couple, open. The firefighter keeps working on following turns until it's done, unless they walk away. Water reaches the engine the turn after the hydrant opens, then refills 8 units a turn. Attack lines are blue.
    • Attack lines come from the hose connections halfway down each long side of an engine: a red 1¾″ and a blue 2½″ on each side. Stand beside the connections with a firefighter selected and click the coupling you want (or press A / D). The 1¾″ uses 1 water per spray, knocks fire down 2 levels and reaches 3 tiles. The 2½″ uses 2 water, knocks it down 3, cools much more and reaches 4, but every new tile of 2½″ hose costs +1 AP to lay out. The hose follows your path, one tile of hose per tile. Each engine carries 28 tiles of hose. Walk back along it to take it back in, put it down for someone else, or pack it back onto the truck. A door with a hose through it can't be closed.
    • The crew rides in seats on the truck, front seats first. Click a firefighter on the truck to select them, then click a tile next to the truck to get them off.
    • One person per tile. Movement and actions share the firefighter's AP for the turn. Furniture costs extra to climb over; cabinets, shelves and trees block.
    • Engine crews (yellow) have 4 AP. Spray reaches 3 tiles in a straight, unobstructed line. Ladder crews (orange) have 5 AP and can raise a ladder below an upper-floor window. Their truck carries no water or hose.
    • Closed doors hold fire back. Heat rises: fire below weakens the floor above and climbs the stairwell. Dashed orange borders mark tiles close to igniting.
    • Victims are hidden until found. Firefighters spot anyone within 3 tiles through clear air. Smoke blinds them, so search tile by tile (Search, 1 AP, or 2 AP in thick smoke). A small green mark shows searched tiles. Carry victims outside; smoke and heat hurt them every turn.
    • Smoke slows everything. Moving through thick smoke costs +1 AP. Spraying from smoke costs +1 AP, or +2 in thick smoke, where the range also drops to 2.
    • Ladder crews do forcible entry and ventilation. Use the Tools mode to force a locked door (2 AP), axe through drywall, a door or a window, or cut a roof vent (3 AP, while on the roof). Ground ladders reach upper windows and the roof.
    • Ventilation: open windows let smoke out, and a roof vent pulls smoke and heat out of the whole space below it. A fan set at an open door blows the smoke out through another opening. But if anything in its path is still burning, the fan feeds the fire. Knock the fire down first.
    • Keys: 1–6 modes, Tab next firefighter, Enter end turn, Z undo, A 1¾″ attack line, D 2½″ attack line, S supply line, N pick up/put down hose, B pack hose, Y hook up hydrant, E search, P place/remove fan, G put person down, L ladder, R turn truck, H/V overlays, Esc cancel.
