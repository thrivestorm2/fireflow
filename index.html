    How to play
    • Everything is a tap (or click) on the map. Tap a firefighter to select them, then tap where you want them to go or what you want them to work on. If there's one obvious thing to do, they do it. If there are several (open a door or axe through it, spray or step in), a small menu lists them with their AP cost.
    • Tap the selected firefighter for jobs right where they stand: search, raise a ground ladder, put the hose down or pack it away, put a person down.
    • Trucks arrive over several turns and wait in Staging. Tap a staging truck in Dispatch, then a road or driveway tile to park it (Rotate turns it). Tap a seated crew member, then a tile beside the truck to get them off.
    • Hoses: stand next to a truck's coupling and tap it: the red 1¾″ or blue 2½″ attack lines halfway down an engine, the yellow 5″ supply coupling at the back. Carry the supply line to a hydrant and tap the hydrant, or to another truck's side inlet and tap that. With an attack line, tap fire to spray it.
    • The aerial: step onto a ladder truck's turntable, view an upper floor and tap open air or roof to raise it there. Tap the tip to climb it; tap fire for the master stream.
    • Fog of war: inside, you only see what your crew can see or feel. Watch the smoke and fire showing at the windows, and switch to the thermal view (🌡️ above the floor selector) to read heat through smoke near a lieutenant.
    • Need more help? Strike the next alarm at the bottom of Dispatch. End the turn when you're done: the fire moves only in the fire phase.
