    How to play
    • Each turn the fire acts first: heat spreads, things ignite, smoke drifts, the structure weakens. Then your crew acts.
    • Select a firefighter (Tab or click) and click tiles. Auto mode moves, sprays fire, opens doors and picks up people.
    • Each firefighter has 4 AP and a 6-shot water tank. Refill next to the engine E.
    • Spray reaches 3 tiles in a straight, unobstructed line. Wet tiles resist ignition for 2 turns.
    • Closed doors hold fire back. Heat rises: fire below weakens the floor above and climbs the stairwell.
    • Dashed orange borders mark tiles close to igniting. Upper floors can collapse — anyone on them falls.
    • Carry civilians out to the street. Smoke and heat hurt them every turn.
    • Keys: 1–6 modes, Tab next firefighter, Enter end turn, Z undo, G drop, R refill, H/V overlays.
