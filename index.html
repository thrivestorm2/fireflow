    How to play
    • Every turn has two phases. In the fire phase heat spreads, things ignite, smoke drifts and the structure weakens. Fire never spreads during your phase.
    • Trucks arrive over several turns. When one is on scene, click Park, then click a road or driveway tile (R rotates). An engine parked within 2 tiles of a hydrant gets unlimited water.
    • The crew starts aboard. Select a firefighter and click a tile next to their truck to get them off.
    • One person per tile. Movement and actions share the firefighter's AP for the turn. Furniture costs extra to climb over; cabinets, shelves and trees block.
    • Engine crews (yellow) have 4 AP and a 6-shot hose. Spray reaches 3 tiles in a straight, unobstructed line; refill next to an engine. Ladder crews (orange) have 5 AP and no hose; they can raise a ladder below an upper-floor window.
    • Closed doors hold fire back. Heat rises: fire below weakens the floor above and climbs the stairwell. Dashed orange borders mark tiles close to igniting.
    • Carry civilians outside. Smoke and heat hurt them every turn.
    • Keys: 1–6 modes, Tab next firefighter, Enter end turn, Z undo, G drop, R refill / rotate truck, L ladder, H/V overlays, Esc cancel.
